SCENE I

Exterior. Day.

SCENE I is the first public threshold of ESCENA. It introduces the house through its most open atmosphere: air, mineral light, salt and morning space.

The camera is inside the event. Not landscape. Not tourist image. A physical situation from within. SCENE I does not describe the exterior — it is inside it.

SCENE I is composed of three material elements organized as a single set.

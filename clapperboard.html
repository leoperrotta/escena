CLAPPERBOARD

The reduced threshold of the house.

CLAPPERBOARD is the compact entry object of ESCENA. It introduces the system through contrast, structure and first selection.

It is not a full archive and not an assembly object. It is the reduced threshold of the house.

CLAPPERBOARD is built as a precise entry point between isolated fragrance release and full roll discovery.

The Screen
Why it exists

Some entry points are too narrow. Others are too complete.

CLAPPERBOARD sits between them.

It allows a first reading of ESCENA through contrast, structure, sequence and controlled sampling.

It is not the full map of the roll. It is the first valid threshold of the system.

Object logic

CLAPPERBOARD is not a literal prop. The cinematic reference appears in the opening mechanism, not in direct reproduction.

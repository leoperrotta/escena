ROLL I

The first complete chapter of ESCENA.

ROLL I is the first production unit of ESCENA. It comprises THE SCREEN and SCENE I through SCENE V.

What first appeared through selected scenes now becomes legible as a sequence. Each scene is a constructed space composed of material elements.

The roll is not a collection. It is a chapter.

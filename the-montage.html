THE MONTAGE

Composition, not collection.

THE MONTAGE is not a discovery format. It is a composition system. Six vials and the instruments for working with them: the DIRECTOR'S NOTEBOOK and the GUIDE CARDS.

In film, montage is the editing of images into sequence — the meaning emerges not from any single frame, but from the relationship between frames. THE MONTAGE operates the same way on skin.

ESCENA provides the elements. THE MONTAGE provides the grammar for their combination.

DIRECTOR'S NOTEBOOK

Composition guide

The system of layering. How the scenes relate, how THE SCREEN functions as ground, and how to construct a personal narrative from the material.

GUIDE CARDS

Usage grammar

Six cards, one per element. Each card maps a scene's material elements, its position in the system, and its behavior in combination.

How The Montage Works

Cut, layer, compose.

THE SCREEN is always the ground. A SCENE is applied over it. A second SCENE may be added — before or after — to construct a transition, a contrast, a sequence.

ESCENA does not prescribe the combinations. The DIRECTOR'S NOTEBOOK documents the logic of the system. The outcome belongs to the wearer.

Step 1Apply THE SCREEN. Establish the field.
Step 2Apply a SCENE. Introduce the figure.
Step 3Wait. Observe. Add or remove.
Step 4Layer a second SCENE if the grammar allows.
ResultA personal sequence. An olfactory edit.

Note

The vocabulary of ESCENA does not include top, heart, or base.

2026

The traditional vocabulary of perfumery describes a temporal sequence: top notes, heart notes, base notes. ESCENA does not use this language. There are material elements. They are present or they are not.

Note

SCENE IV: the rain is not romantic.

2026

The rain in SCENE IV is not a decorative effect. It is a physical condition — weight, surface, saturation. The exterior entering the interior through condensation.

Note

On ESCENA not directing the experience.

2026

ESCENA constructs material spaces and provides the language for composing. It does not direct the experience of the wearer. The narrative emerges in the encounter between the material and the person.

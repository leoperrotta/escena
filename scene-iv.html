Interior. Rain.

SCENE IV is condensation, humidity, leaves, wet floor, suspension. The exterior entering the interior through rain.

The rain is not romanticized. It is not a decorative effect. It is a physical condition — weight, surface, saturation.

SCENE IV is composed of three material elements organized as a single set.
